About

Philosophy and Background

Hi, I’m Bill. I’m a Licensed Clinical Social Worker in Pennsylvania, and TherapyPA is my solo virtual practice. I work with adults whose lives look steady from the outside while something underneath is not: grief that has not loosened its grip, anxiety that keeps the mind running, and the particular exhaustion of people who are used to holding it together for everyone else.

My approach is unhurried and conversational. Patterns we carry, in how we grieve, work, and relate to the people closest to us, were usually learned early and for good reasons. They run quietly, though, and they shape choices long after the reasons have changed. In therapy we slow down enough to notice those patterns, understand where they came from, and loosen the ones that no longer serve you.

Self-understanding is not the end goal. The aim is to put it to work: grieving without getting stuck in it, working without burning out, and being in your relationships in ways that feel more honest and more sustainable.

I am licensed in Pennsylvania, and all sessions are held by secure video. My practice focuses on the five-county Philadelphia area: Philadelphia, Bucks, Chester, Delaware, and Montgomery counties.

On a personal note, I am a lifelong Philadelphian and a runner. I live here with my family, and most weekends you can find me on one of the city’s running routes with a camera in hand.

Frequently asked questions

What is your psychotherapy fee?

For self-pay, it is $200 per session. Payment is by credit or debit card through the secure client portal, or by Venmo. Depending on availability, I may be able to offer a reduced fee based on financial need.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. A free 20-minute consultation so we can see if we’re a good fit. We’ll talk about what brings you in, any questions you have, and what working together would look like.

Where and how do sessions happen?

All sessions are held online through secure telehealth, for adults located in Pennsylvania.

Do you take insurance?

Not yet. I am currently credentialing with Independence Blue Cross (IBX) for in-network therapy. Until credentialing clears, sessions are private pay. I provide superbills (a detailed receipt) you can submit to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement.

How does a superbill work?

You pay me directly. I provide a superbill on request. You submit it to your insurance company. Your insurance may reimburse part of the cost.

How will I know if my insurance takes superbills?

You will have to call your insurance company and ask the following questions: Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits? Is prior authorization required? Do I need to pay an annual deductible first? What is the reimbursement rate? Are there any session limits per year? What information is needed on the superbill? How do I submit the superbill (online, mail, app)? How long does it take to be reimbursed?

Do I need a referral or preauthorization?

No. For private-pay therapy, no referral or preauthorization is needed. If you plan to use out-of-network benefits, your insurer may have its own requirements, so it is worth a quick call to them first.

What is the first session like?

We’ll talk about what brings you in, what you want to be different, and how we might work together. There’s no pressure to have it all figured out. Beginning where you are is the whole point.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed sessions are charged the full fee, except in true emergencies.
