How it works

Therapy that meets you
where you are.

We meet over secure telehealth. You pick a quiet spot, at home, in your office, wherever works, and we talk. For most people it works just as well as sitting in the same room, and it means you get to choose a therapist you actually connect with, not just whoever happens to be nearby.

I’m licensed in Pennsylvania and work with adults in the Philadelphia suburbs. If you’re in Montgomery County and looking for a therapist who gets it, we can start with a free 20-minute consultation and see if we’re a good fit.

What I help with

You don’t have to
carry it alone.

01

Traumatic grief

When loss comes with trauma, a sudden death, a violent loss, grief tangled up with what happened, the mourning needs room, and so does the shock. I work with people carrying both.

02

High-achiever burnout

From the outside it looks like success. Inside it feels like running on empty with no permission to stop. I work with high achievers who are burning out and want a different relationship with work, ambition, and rest.

03

Men’s mental health

A lot of men were never given much language for what’s happening inside. Therapy can be a place to build that language, without judgment and without having to perform being fine.

04

First responders

Police, fire, EMS, dispatch: the job asks you to absorb what most people never see. I spent a decade in the victim services field and community behavioral health. I understand the culture and the cost.

05

Anxiety & depression

Racing thoughts, dread that won’t lift, the flat gray of depression. Practical tools and deeper understanding, together. Not one or the other.

06

PTSD & trauma

Trauma-informed care including Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT), structured, evidence-based work for when the past won’t stay in the past.

About Bill

I’m Bill Curtin, a licensed clinical social worker based in Philadelphia. I work with adults through online therapy in the Philadelphia suburbs, including Montgomery County.

I hold a Master of Social Work from the University of Pennsylvania and am a Licensed Clinical Social Worker in Pennsylvania (license CW025367, current through February 28, 2027). Before private practice, I spent a decade in the victim services field and community behavioral health.

Questions

What people
usually ask.

Do I need to be in Montgomery County for this to work?

No. All sessions are online through secure telehealth, so anywhere in the Philly suburbs works. You just need a private spot and an internet connection.

What are your fees?

Sessions are $200. A limited number of reduced-fee spots are available based on financial need. I provide superbills on request if you’d like to seek out-of-network reimbursement from your insurer.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. A free 20-minute consultation so we can see if we’re a good fit. We’ll talk about what brings you in, any questions you have, and what working together would look like.

Do you take insurance?

Not yet. I am currently credentialing with Independence Blue Cross (IBX) for in-network therapy, and I expect to be able to bill IBX plans once credentialing clears. Until then, sessions are $200, private pay, with superbills available if you would like to seek out-of-network reimbursement. If you have an IBX plan and want to be first in line, book a free consultation and mention IBX to join the waitlist.
